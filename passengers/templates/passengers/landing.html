How to use this website

From login to forecast in 7 steps

Follow these steps to load data, explore trends, run a forecast and export the results. Every step below links straight to the page you need.

1
Create an account

Open Register and fill in username, email and password. Django validates the password strength, then signs you in automatically and takes you to the dashboard.

Password validators CSRF protected
2
Log in to your account

Already registered? Use Log in. Sessions keep you signed in, and every data page is protected — visiting a protected URL without a session redirects you to the login page automatically.

Session based Logout from the top bar
3
Load your passenger data

Go to Import CSV, drag your file into the drop zone and press Preview to validate the first 20 rows. When it looks right, press Import. CSV format: Month,Passengers (e.g. 1949-01,112). A sample dataset of 144 months is already loaded.

Duplicates skipped Preview first Max 5 MB
4
Read the dashboard

The Dashboard shows the headline numbers: total passengers, monthly average, peak & low months, growth rate and the predicted value for the next month. Below them: trend line, yearly bars, seasonal averages, YoY growth and the heatmap.

Live charts Heatmap
5
Explore trends

Open Trend Analysis for monthly seasonality, yearly totals, MoM & YoY growth, peak/low years and a full timeline. Use Visualizations for the expanded chart studio, including the interactive heatmap and a forecast overlay.

Seasonal index MoM / YoY
6
Generate a forecast

On Forecasting pick a model (Linear, Moving Average or Seasonal), set how many months to project and press Generate Forecast. You get the predicted table, the historical-vs-forecast chart and the accuracy metrics. Moving Average also exposes a window setting.

Real model output Download CSV JSON API
7
Export & report

Use Reports for a printable executive summary, or download the raw data with Export CSV on the Passenger Data page — the export respects whatever filters you have applied.

Print / PDF CSV export

Under the hood

Real analysis, not fake numbers

Every figure on this site is calculated by Python from the stored dataset — there are no hard-coded statistics anywhere in the templates.

01
Descriptive statistics

Total, mean, max, min, count, peak and low records per year, computed with Pandas groupby.

02
Seasonal index

Each month's average divided by the overall average, expressed as a percentage index.

03
MoM & YoY growth

Month-over-month percentage change and year-over-year totals, with a base year for the first period.

04
CAGR

Compound annual growth rate derived from the first and last records of the dataset.

05
Time-series models

Linear Regression, Moving Average and Seasonal Exponential Smoothing, evaluated on a 20% test split.

06
Accuracy metrics

MAE, RMSE, MAPE and R² per model so you can compare which approach fits your data best.
